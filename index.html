Auction Arena

Where Strategy Meets Bidding

Enter a strategic auction where your private valuation guides your bidding decision. Compete against three computer bidders and win items by making smart bids.

WHAT IS THE GAME?

Auction Arena is a sealed-bid auction where each bidder submits a bid without seeing other bidders' bids. The highest bidder wins, but pays the second-highest bid rather than their own bid.

GAME OBJECTIVE

Your goal is not simply to win auctions. Make strategic bids that generate positive utility by balancing your private valuation, competition, and the payment you may make if you win.

WHAT MAKES THIS GAME STRATEGIC?

Winning an auction is not the only goal. Your performance also depends on how much value you gain from each successful bid.
